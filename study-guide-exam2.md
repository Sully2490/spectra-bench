# Study Guide for Exam 2 Gen Chem 1045-3

## Chapter 3
- [ ] Nature of light/EM radiation: wave/particle duality, nodes, wave equation
- [ ] Nature of the electron: standing wave, electron probability, Heisenberg uncertainty principle
- [ ] Explanation of H atom emission lines
- [ ] Principal quantum number n (1, 2, 3...)
- [ ] Angular momentum quantum number l (0 to n-1)
- [ ] Magnetic quantum number ml (-l to +l)
- [ ] Spin quantum number (+1/2, -1/2); identify an element from quantum numbers in an s or p orbital
- [ ] Orbital shapes s, p, d (skip f)
- [ ] Pauli exclusion principle
- [ ] Orbital diagrams and Hund's rule
- [ ] Filling order (1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d...)
- [ ] Electron configuration and abbreviated (noble gas) configuration
- [ ] Periodic trends: atomic radius, ionization energy, electron affinity

## Chapter 4
- [ ] Nature of the covalent bond (sharing)
- [ ] Ionic vs polar covalent vs nonpolar covalent from the electronegativity table
- [ ] Nomenclature of ionic/covalent species, polyatomics
- [ ] Lewis structures of elements (dot symbols)
- [ ] Lewis structures of covalent molecules with single, double, triple bonds
- [ ] Octet rule violators (fewer or more)
- [ ] Formal charges (formula given)
- [ ] Resonance structures and evaluating them
- [ ] Electron geometry and molecular geometry
- [ ] VSEPR and lone pair repulsion
- [ ] Bond angles in 3-D
- [ ] Bond dipoles canceling in symmetric structures
